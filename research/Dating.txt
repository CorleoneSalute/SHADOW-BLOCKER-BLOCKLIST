Together Networks

Together Networks is a network of more than 300 dating sites (Flirt.com, BeNaughty, Loveaholics, and hundreds of regional/niche variants).

It was investigated by Snopes in 2022 in a series of reports.

Snopes documented a rotating set of shell billing companies used across the network (non-dating domains such as nlvfee.com or plffee.com), specifically domains used to create an unrecognizable charge on the card statement.

Users report being moved from a trial period into recurring payments automatically, being cross-enrolled on other sites in the same network without giving explicit consent, and receiving message traffic that is largely bot-generated.
Because of all this, the known Together Networks domains and the billing shell domains linked to them are fully blocked on my list.
